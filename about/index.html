Why this library exists. A PII tool usually answers one question: is this document sensitive? That answer is too coarse for a support ticket, a log line, or a prompt, and it gives no proof of what left the system. The smallest honest fix is a typed range of characters on the caller's own string, with one checkable equation behind every row. So he built it, and then measured it on two public corpora rather than trusting a model card.

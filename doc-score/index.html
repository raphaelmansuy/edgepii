First public score

This file records the first score of the shipped lanes on a public corpus. It answers one question: does the library find PII, and where does it fail?

The run date is 4 November 2026. The host is a MacBook with an arm64 CPU and no accelerator. Both corpora are synthetic. Every number here is a measurement on that host, on those bytes. A number is not a ship gate. The floors stay open until a sealed freeze exists.

What this shows

The domain transfer is the finding, not the average. PERSON scores 0.995 on the corpus the checkpoint trained on and 0.048 on a finance document. The model did not learn a grammar of names. It learned where a name sits in a synthetic persona note. A card figure of 0.9628 would have hidden that.

The model carries the structured types that a rule cannot reach. GOV_ID scores 1.000 on Nemotron where the rule lane scores 0.000, because v1 ships no national id pattern. The two lanes are complementary, and the preset already runs both and merges them.

Precision is the cost of recall. en-closed reaches recall 0.987 by masking almost every capitalised token. ADDRESS precision is 0.237 and PHONE precision is 0.472 on Nemotron. The caller pays for that utility, and min_score is the only lever the library offers, because the library ships no floor.

The rule lane is a floor, not a product. Its PAYMENT score is 0.091 on Nemotron. Of the 251 false positive span matches in the 2 000 document run, 150 are text that holds no gold label at all. The samples are dates such as 2028-07-15 and bare account numbers such as 5482370169. Both pass _luhn_ok, because ISO/IEC 7812-1 sets the minimum PAN length at 8 digits and a date in ISO 8601 form is 8 digits once the separators are dropped. A further 26 are a real DATE_OF_BIRTH and 56 are a real PHONE, so the card rule is reaching into two other public types. Only 19 are a US SSN.

The taxonomy rank puts GOV_ID above PAYMENT, so a correct GOV_ID hit wins the merge. It cannot help here, because the rule lane has no SSN rule at all, for the reason the rule audit gives: no national id pattern ships in v1. The card rule also cannot tell an 8-digit date from a PAN. That is a real gap, and this corpus made it visible.

Two leak counts matter more than either F1. en-closed leaks 2 high-severity spans on 500 Nemotron documents and 38 on 400 Gretel documents. The parent product note says a leakage rate above zero blocks a ship. Neither preset is ready to ship on a finance domain, and the number says why.

What to do next

  1. Decide whether an 8-digit PAN is worth keeping. ISO/IEC 7812-1 allows one, but a date in ISO 8601 form is also 8 digits, and 150 of the 251 PAYMENT false positives are exactly that. The rule has a correct source and a bad cost on real text.
  2. Read twenty PERSON misses on Gretel. The sample is a SWIFT field and a signature block, not prose. That is the shape a Lane G preset should be tested against.
  3. Add multi-open to the same runs. A span model with a type list at call time is the lane the escalation ladder names after en-closed.
  4. Keep the two corpora apart. A Nemotron score and a Gretel score are two numbers about two corpora. Do not average them into one.

None of these steps changes a floor. The floors wait for the sealed freeze.

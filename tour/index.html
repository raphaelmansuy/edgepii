Screenshot tour

Every screen, and what it proves

These images are produced by the browser test run, not by hand. The test fills the page, clicks Locate spans, waits for the result, and writes the file. A changed image means a changed behaviour. Regenerate them with make e2e.

How to read a screenshot

  • A green slice check card and a green ok in each row mean every span passed source[start:end] == text.
  • A red mark is a contract failure, not a style issue.
  • A dash in the Score column means no validator covers that form. The library does not fill the gap with a number.
  • The span table is the review artifact. A masked paragraph alone is not.
